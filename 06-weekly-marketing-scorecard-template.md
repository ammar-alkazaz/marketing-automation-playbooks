# 06. Weekly marketing scorecard

One page, every week, same layout. Management reads it in two minutes and knows what the budget bought.

## Layout

**Headline.** One sentence on the week: what moved and why.

**Numbers table (per channel and total).**

| Channel | Spend | Clicks | Leads | Qualified leads | Cost per lead | Cost per qualified lead | Booked calls |
|---------|-------|--------|-------|-----------------|---------------|-------------------------|--------------|

**Trend.** The same table for the previous four weeks, so a change is visible without commentary.

**Learning-phase caveat.** When a campaign is new, say so on the same page and give the date when the numbers become judgeable.

**What changed this week.** Three bullets at most. Tests started, tests ended, settings changed.

**Next week.** Three bullets at most. What will be tested and what decision it will inform.

**Lead quality note.** One line from sales. The numbers above are only as good as the leads behind them.

## Rules

- Spend, clicks, leads, cost per lead and booked calls only. No vanity metrics (impressions, CPM, reach) unless they explain a cost change.
- Numbers come from the CRM for leads and from the platforms for spend. Never the platform's own lead count alone.
- Statistical caveats sit on the page, not in a footnote nobody reads.
- The file name carries the week ending date.
