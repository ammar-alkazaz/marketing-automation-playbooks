# 07. Content system templates

The pieces that let founder content and company content run without depending on one person's memory. Fill them in once, hand them over with the content.

## Voice guide (founder profile)

- Three sentences on how this person talks.
- Five things they always do (specific stories, a contrarian take, a genuine closing question).
- Five things they never do (engagement bait, hedging, stacked adjectives, jargon).
- Three examples of posts that performed, with one line on why.

## Verified fact bank

A table of facts the content may use: the claim, the source, the date verified, who verified it. Nothing goes into a post unless it is in the table. This is the single rule that keeps ghostwriting safe.

## Content pillars and rotation

Four pillars, one post per pillar per week, rotated so the same pillar never runs twice in a row. Example set for a services founder: contrarian take on the industry, practical take on a tool or trend, human and local story, client or mission spotlight.

## Monthly calendar

| Week | Day | Pillar | Hook shape | Status | Owner | Link |
|------|-----|--------|------------|--------|-------|------|

Hook shapes rotate (a familiar moment, a cost already happening, a broken belief, a question to the reader is not a hook).

## Batch ticket (short-form video)

- Episode ID (series, batch number, episode number, topic, CTA code)
- Script body (one lesson, a payoff line, then one CTA from the locked bank)
- Cover text (also the posting title)
- Overlay idea (one literal cause and effect)
- Platform exports needed
- Review status and reviewer

## Editing playbook skeleton

- Project settings (frame size, frame rate, export presets per platform)
- Grade (reference values, one look for the whole batch)
- Captions (font, size, colour, emphasis rule, safe zone)
- Audio (target loudness, peak ceiling, check on a phone speaker)
- Punch-ins and pacing rule
- Outro and cover rules
- Naming and delivery folder

## Testing rule

Seventy percent of each batch repeats the proven format. Thirty percent tests one variable (length, hook shape, CTA). One variable per batch, measured against the same window.

## Handoff checklist

Voice guide, fact bank, calendar, the batch ticket template, the editing playbook, the CTA bank, the naming convention and the review flow, all in one folder, with the owner's name on each file.
